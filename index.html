Appliance Assessment and Recovery Options

Kitchen appliances represent significant investment and may be salvageable depending on fire proximity and damage severity. Refrigerators and freezers often survive kitchen fires without significant damage because they are typically not near the fire origin. However, any appliance that has been exposed to heat or smoke requires professional assessment before use. Gas ranges and cooktops are sometimes damaged internally even if external appearance seems unburned. Electric ovens and cooktops may have damaged internal components that make them unsafe to operate. In Mount Vernon, appliance manufacturers sometimes recommend complete replacement rather than attempted repair if internal electrical or gas components have been compromised.
